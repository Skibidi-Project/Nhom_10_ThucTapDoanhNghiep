# Final-period billing: design and release blockers

Review only. The contract model currently has monthly rent, start/end dates and payment cycle, but no structured final-period rent method, rounding rule, inclusive-day rule, service stop date, historical effective price, or meter-replacement event. Do not infer such terms or backdate current property prices. The existing monthly US-22 invoice is not an approved final-period invoice.

The isolated-only trial records manual requests for rent, electricity, water and each assigned service, with proposed method and amount, reason/reference, input data, requester and landlord decision. An assigned manager can request but not approve financial decisions. A separate trial endpoint consumes one valid approval per required item and writes an invoice, snapshot and approval state in one transaction. The ordinary invoice endpoint refuses periods with trial requests. The request includes reading revision to detect meter ABA. Trial application writers of property prices, service prices and assignments increment a property input revision and serialize with the trial workflow. This is not durable historical pricing: direct SQL, older application instances and writers without the trial flag do not follow that protocol. Therefore this trial must not be promoted to production. A revised request can be created when captured input changes, retaining the old one.

Before release, specify and approve: contract and service terms as versioned immutable facts, effective-dated prices, meter identifiers and meter replacement continuity, revisioned corrections of handover records, exact usage interval and overlap prevention, rounding, historical invoice adjustments, and financial roles. The trial handover references existing US-19 readings with their revisions and records confirmation time, but cannot handle a meter reset or reconfirm a stale handover. Every writer of referenced inputs must participate in a shared locking/versioning protocol, not only the new API. Absence of a historical price or rule must remain an explicit unresolved item; a manual amount must not be silently interpreted as an automatic price.

Proposed adjustment model, not migration or executable functionality: append-only adjustment header referencing original invoice and immutable snapshot checksum, signed amount/currency, reason, attachments/references, creator/time, approver/time, and lifecycle; append-only item lines referencing original items and effective interval. Use a unique application/idempotency key, lock contract/room and original invoice with payment records in a consistent order, validate an approved adjustment against the current payment balance, and atomically record balance impact. Never mutate original invoice or original snapshot. Credit, overpayment, refund and negative-balance policy must be approved before implementation.

Trial tables in `us16_settlement_trial.sql` are prohibited on production. Production startup still calls automatic DDL; disable/control it through an approved deployment change and validate schema before enabling application traffic. Review `INTEGRATION_REVIEW.md` for backup, rollback and snapshot deployment limits. No new adjustment or meter-handover DDL has been applied.
